Little Explorer and the Rainbow Bridge

🐻 🌈 🐰

Little Explorer met Pillu by a little stream.

“Let’s build a bridge!” said Mira.

Little Explorer gave Fenni a red block. Beaky added a blue one.

Keyboard made a tiny star shine. The friends clapped. “We did it together!”

Try together: point to something red. Clap your hands. Say “together!”

Goodnight, Little Explorer

🌙 ⭐ 🐧

The sky grew quiet. Little Explorer looked up.

“One little star,” whispered Beaky.

Pillu waved to the trees. Mira closed her book. Fenni put the map away.

Keyboard glowed softly. “Goodnight, Little Explorer. Tomorrow brings another little wonder.”

Try together: wave goodnight. Take a slow breath. Give a cozy cuddle.
