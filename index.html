Good things to know

For curious parents.

Who is this learning world for?

Gen Babies is designed for ages 2–6, with a grown-up nearby. Choose activities based on your child’s interests and comfort rather than a fixed pace.

Can children actually play games and read stories on the Gen Babies™ website?

Yes! Gen Babies™ offers interactive discovery games that children can play directly on our website, along with sample stories to explore. You can also visit our official YouTube channel to discover our videos and learning adventures.

How do sound and animation work?

Sound starts off. Turn it on for gentle feedback tones in the games. Pause animation anytime, or use bedtime mode to pause it automatically. Your device’s reduced-motion setting is respected.

Does this website save my child’s information?

This website has no sign-in, tracking code or child profiles. The only form is the optional Contact page, which emails your message to our support team. Activity progress stays in the current page and resets when you refresh. Fonts load from Google Fonts; opening YouTube takes you to that service.
